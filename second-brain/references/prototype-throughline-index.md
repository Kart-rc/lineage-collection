# Throughline Prototype: PRD Index

The front door of the Throughline document suite (Product Requirements v0.9, internal draft, "not for
distribution"). Its one-paragraph framing of the platform: end-to-end lineage capture and impact
analysis — one trusted, column-level lineage graph across services, streams, S3, and file transfers
that turns any upstream schema change into a pre-merge impact question. Three PRDs and a working
prototype.

The index organizes the suite by reading role:

- **Start here (leadership)** — the Executive Narrative: the four-act arc from "every change is a
  blind bet" to the funding decision, plus talk track and pre-answered leadership questions.
- **The three documents** — PRD 1 (Collection), PRD 2 (Impact), PRD 3 (UI/UX), each with a card
  summary and topic chips.
- **Delivery plan** — Roadmap & LOE: the 24-month plan sequencing the PRDs into one build with
  critical path vs parallel work, a quarter-by-quarter Gantt, per-workstream effort, team capacity,
  and timeline-compression levers.
- **Technical reference** — Architecture & Scale: four-plane architecture, process flows,
  REST + GraphQL surface, schemas, and sizing for tens of thousands of services.
- **Walkthrough** — Worked Example: two chained scenarios in which one money-type change ripples
  six hops across two teams.
- **Planning tool** — Onboarding Estimator: an interactive model quoting time-to-onboard a vertical
  slice via archetypes, collector mappings, and a connector-reuse learning curve, preloaded with the
  Orders slice.
- **Pre-leadership review and response** — Critical Review (red-team read) and Remediation Plan
  (accepted findings turned into owned, dated actions: business value every 6-week PI, capacity
  reconciliation, the P0 identity-resolution spike, an adoption workstream, leading indicators).
- **Reference prototype** — the working four-lens interactive app the PRDs describe: altitude
  navigation, progressive disclosure to column level, the interaction matrix, confidence-scored
  provenance, and impact blast radius.
